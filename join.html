The machine behind the cell

FC26 is the first system built for this.

FC26 is an early-warning system for elections—the first machine purpose-built to separate coordinated disinformation and inauthentic behavior from ordinary discourse, trace who’s behind it, and anticipate its next move before it spreads. A multi-stage AI pipeline filters the firehose by keyword and geography—down to your counties—then reads each signal for intent: who is doing what to whom, not just the words it uses. Every signal is scored against a fixed rubric and filed as a structured Threat & Influence Report. The cell acts on that—signal, not noise.

Honest about the line

Not activism. Democracy with better tools.

The fastest way to lose trust is to blur this line. So we won’t. Here’s exactly what the cell does—and what it refuses to do.

✓ What we do

  • Flag coordinated, false claims that could actually change what someone does—like a real polling place falsely reported as closed.
  • Check them against official local sources and put out accurate info where people are confused.
  • Say how sure we are—and admit our blind spots—instead of acting certain.
  • Keep a human in charge of every call that matters.

× What we don’t do

  • We have no link to the social media companies—we can’t delete posts or block anyone. We’re a radar, not a referee.
  • We don’t review viewpoints. Frustration, long lines, weather complaints, and political debate are protected by design.
  • We don’t decide what’s true on anyone’s behalf—disclosure, not censorship.
  • We’re non-partisan. Full stop.
